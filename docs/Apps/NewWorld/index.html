QUICK START / HOW TO PLAY

  1. Create or join a worldOpen MULTIPLAYER. The host creates a private room and shares its code.
  2. Start the frontierOnce everyone is in the founding party, the host presses START WORLD.
  3. Claim a regionSelect an UNCLAIMED State on the map, then claim it for ¤450. Your new settlement receives a development reserve.
  4. Build your settlementDouble-click your region, choose an object, press BUILD, then click an empty dry plot.
  5. Expand and manageUse ECONOMY, GOVERNMENT, MILITARY and RESEARCH to grow, negotiate and compete.
